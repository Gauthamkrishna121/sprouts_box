Sprouts Box was founded with a simple yet powerful mission: to restore the bond between people and pure, unadulterated food.

We partner directly with small-scale local farmers in Kerala who practice regenerative, eco-friendly agriculture. By eliminating middle traders, we ensure our farmers earn fair wages while bringing you produce harvested at peak ripeness, completely free from harmful pesticides, artificial waxes, or chemicals.

Our Core Values —

What Makes Sprouts Box Different

Zero Harmful Chemicals

Every single vegetable and fruit is grown using natural compost and organic pest management. No toxic sprays ever.

Fair Support for Farmers

We empower local farming communities by providing fair prices and long-term sustainable livelihood guarantees.

Same-Day Harvest Delivery

Your orders are picked directly from our partner fields at dawn and packed with care to reach your doorstep fresh.

Eco-Friendly Packaging

We say no to single-use plastics. All produce is packed in biodegradable jute sacks, brown paper, and wooden boxes.
