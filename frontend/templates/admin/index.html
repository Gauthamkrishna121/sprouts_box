💡 Quick Guide: How to Delete Products or Categories

1. One-Click Row Delete Open any product or category list and click the red 🗑️ Delete button right in the table row.
2. Bulk Deletion Select checkboxes next to multiple items, choose "Delete selected" from the action dropdown, and click Go.
3. Inside Edit View Click on any product or category to edit, then click the red Delete button at the bottom of the form.
